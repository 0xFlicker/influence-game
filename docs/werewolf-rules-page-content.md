# Werewolf Rules

The House presents Werewolf. A village of agents talks by day while a hidden pack attacks at night. Win with your faction—even if you are eliminated.

## How to Win

The **village wins when every werewolf is eliminated**. The **wolves win when they equal or outnumber the other living players**. Victory is checked after the full day or night resolution. If neither faction wins before the configured day limit, the game is a draw.

## Players and Roles

Choose **6–8 players**, **one or two werewolves**, and whether to include a **Seer** and a **Doctor**. The remaining roles are Villagers. Roles are shuffled secretly when the game starts. Character archetypes describe personality and strategy; they do not determine secret roles.

| Role | Ability |
| --- | --- |
| Werewolf | Knows the pack and helps choose a nightly attack. Bluffs and false role claims are legal. |
| Villager | Uses conversation and votes to find the wolves. Has no night action. |
| Seer | Investigates exactly one other living player each night and privately learns whether they are a wolf. |
| Doctor | Protects one living player each night, including themself. Cannot protect the same player on consecutive nights. |

## The First Night

Everyone introduces themselves before night one. The pack selects an attack, the Seer investigates if present, and the Doctor protects if present. All choices use the same starting roster and resolve together. Protection stops an attack even when the Doctor is attacked. A Seer who dies still receives the result, but cannot communicate afterward.

## Pack Decisions

With two living wolves, each wolf may make one short proposal, followed by a sealed vote. They must agree on the same target. If they disagree, the pack sees those votes, reverses speaking order, and tries again. There are at most **three votes per night**. Three disagreements mean no attack. A lone wolf chooses directly. The Seer and Doctor still act when the pack fails to agree.

## Daytime Conversation

Opening order is shuffled once for the whole game and rotates across days, skipping eliminated players. Each living player gets at most one opening per day.

The opener chooses **up to three ordered recipients**. Those players respond in that order; the rest of the room follows in a fixed random order. Each respondent chooses Speak or Pass. After every spoken response, the opener may answer or pass and is told who may speak next. A respondent's pass skips that answer. An opening pass skips the thread.

Each accepted contribution becomes public before the next player acts. No one receives future replies. There are no private daytime whispers or alliance huddles. Short performance cues are acting notes, not extra speech or game actions.

## Day Votes

After each thread, all living players—including wolves—submit fresh sealed ballots. Before the final thread, choose another player to eliminate or **Hear more**, an abstention. **More than half of all living players** must choose the same target to end the day immediately. Abstentions count in that denominator. Without a majority, discussion continues.

After everyone has opened, there is a final vote. Every player must choose another living player. **The unique highest vote count eliminates that player, even without a majority. A tie means no daytime elimination.** Normal night actions follow. Ballots reveal together; votes never carry forward and self-votes are illegal. Unavailable model responses are recorded separately from a player's deliberate abstention.

## Seer Investigations

There are **no daytime investigations**. Day one permits at most one completed read, day two at most two, and day N at most N. Another conversation thread or vote does not grant another read. A claimant may reveal fewer results, and claims are not certified by the House. The real Seer's own investigation ledger is certain to them.

## Elimination and Hidden Information

Eliminated players stop speaking, voting, and acting at night. There are no final words, jury, resurrection, or role changes. **Eliminated roles remain hidden until the ending.** Everyone in the winning faction wins, including eliminated members.

## Watching and Visuals

Choose **Mystery** to follow only public conversation and outcomes, or **Omniscient** to know roles and hear pack conversations and resolved night choices. Viewing mode stays fixed for that session. Omniscient viewers can toggle captured thinking; Mystery never receives it.

Visual Mode automatically prepares village and private pack scenes during play. It uses the House production renderer; missing or failed scenes use character art. Producers can inspect cost, review scenes, and publish replacements. Presentation never changes accepted dialogue, votes, or outcomes.

## Character Strategies

Backstory, personality, and visuals are shared across House games. Each game freezes its own strategy at the start. Werewolf uses the character's Werewolf notes, or a Werewolf-specific archetype default when those notes are blank. Influence strategy is never substituted.
