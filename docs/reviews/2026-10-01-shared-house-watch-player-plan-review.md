## Adversarial probes required during implementation

These are specified tests, not claims that the current prototype passed them.

1. **Silent tail:** opening pass, reply pass with cue, unavailable contribution, dozens of consecutive passes, then an early-majority vote or a stopped/live frontier. No Pass frame, runaway fetch loop, repeat announcement or lost thinking cutoff.
2. **Audience race:** delay Omniscient thinking and pack media, switch to Mystery, then resolve the old requests and image readiness callbacks. No old role, image, selected-player data or thought can paint; the toggle resets off.
3. **Same-phase future evidence:** two thoughts by one actor in the same Influence round/phase; rewind between them. Only the earlier evidence is returned/displayed. Repeat within one Werewolf thread and across multiple subcues of a single resolved vote.
4. **Live detach:** seek backward, append new dialogue/results and published media, reconnect with an overlapping window, then Go Live. No automatic catch-up while detached, duplicate speech, reset elapsed time, or unrequested publication switch.
5. **Delayed destination:** rapidly seek A → B → C with B slow and its image completing last. Retain A during preparation; only C may commit. A loading transition must not replay the previous line or stack two titles.
6. **Media coverage:** three or more source groups, failed stitching, a speaker present only in a later verified tile, missing tile and failed frozen portrait. Continue with accepted source imagery or named fallback, without new inference or an indefinite readiness wait.
7. **Result boundaries:** no-majority checkpoint continues the same day, final plurality tie spares everyone, night with no kill, Seer/Doctor already dead, and terminal faction victory. All display facts come from the game adapter and canonical projection.
8. **Input ownership:** keyboard focus in a select, text field, modal or inspector; fullscreen and Escape; reduced motion; background-tab pause. No duplicate listeners, hijacked input or animation callback from a disposed cue.
9. **Release scope:** compare desktop/mobile MCP banner against its existing component and verify `/get-mcp` unchanged. No MCP backend requirement is introduced by the viewer tasks.
